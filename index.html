§ 01

How it works

Plate I / IV
  1. 01 / Describe

    Write one sentence.

    Say what you want to deliver and by when — “Launch a mobile banking app in 3 months.” No template to pick, no form to fill.

  2. 02 / Architect

    The hierarchy is built.

    The AI Project Architect lays out the whole plan in four levels: Project › Structure › Section › Task, with descriptions on each item.

  3. 03 / Council

    The plan is reviewed.

    Four AI roles read the plan in order and attach their findings to the items they concern. You see them inline as Why is this here?

Then It is yours. Rename, move, delete or add anything on a drag-and-drop kanban — nothing the AI built is locked.

§ 02

The Plan Council

Plate II / IV
Fig. II Deliberation

The Plan Council

The reasoning lives next to the work.

Most AI tools hand you an answer and forget why. Beaver keeps the why. Each finding is attached to the item it explains, so six months later the answer to “why was this section split in three?” lives on the section itself.

  1. I Advocate Explains what each part is for, and what would be missing without it.
  2. II Challenger Looks for overlap, wrong ordering and work the plan quietly skips.
  3. III Expert Weighs effort and sequence, and flags what sits on the critical path.
  4. IV Judge Settles disagreements in one sentence — the verdict you read later.

Note The council never rewrites your plan; it justifies it. If one of the roles fails, you still get the full plan — just with fewer annotations.

§ 05

Questions

Appendix
Is there a free plan?

Yes. Starter is free: one user, two projects and the full kanban. AI generation and file uploads are not included on Starter.

How does AI generation work, and what counts as one generation?

You write one prompt. The AI Project Architect builds the full plan, then the Plan Council reviews it and attaches its findings. That whole run — one prompt, one complete reviewed plan — counts as one generation. Up to 10 AI generations per month on Pro, 20 on Business and 100 on Enterprise.

Can I edit what the AI builds?

Yes, everything. Generated items are ordinary items: rename, move, delete or add to them like anything you created yourself.

Who can see my projects?

Only you and the people you invite. Sharing by email is available on the Business plan and up, and access is granted per item — someone assigned to one task sees that task and the path to it, nothing else. Data is stored on Supabase with row-level security.

Can I export my data or use an API?

Not yet — it’s on our roadmap.

How can I pay?

By card, through Polar, our payment provider. We never see or store your card details.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Turn off auto-renew in your profile; your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.
