Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

ZeysLabs (“we”) provides Beaver, a project planning service. This page explains what information is used to provide it.

Information we handle

When you create an account, Supabase Auth handles your email and password or sign-in through Google or GitHub. Project information you add, including prompts and plan content, is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL with row-level security). Uploaded files are stored in Supabase Storage.

If you use plan generation, your prompt and the plan content needed to generate a result are sent through OpenRouter to the language model providers it routes requests to.

Payments are handled by Polar, our merchant of record. Card details are collected by Polar and do not come to Beaver. Our application is hosted on Vercel.

How we use information

We use this information to provide account access, save and display your projects and files, generate plans when requested, and manage subscriptions.

Third-party services

Supabase provides authentication, database, and file storage; OpenRouter routes AI requests to language model providers; Polar handles payments; and Vercel hosts the application. Their handling of information is subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices

You can turn off renewal at any time; your plan stays active until the end of the current period. To ask a question about your information or request deletion, contact us at contact@zeyslabs.com.
